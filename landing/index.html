How it reads

Three steps, and each one can be checked.

Retrieve, then grade what it found

Hybrid search pulls the closest matching passages. A grading pass throws out anything not firmly grounded in the source — a weak document never reaches your screen.

Answer with a score attached

Every answer ships with the passages it used and a confidence value. No silent guesswork: you can tell how sure it is before you trust it.

Pause on the dangerous parts

Deleting an order, issuing a refund, sharing a record — these wait for a human. The graph stops, holds state, and resumes only on your approval or refusal.

Verification

Every claim is a link back to the source.

Who decides

The model proposes. You dispose.

The whole design works around one rule: automation up to the point of risk, never past it.
